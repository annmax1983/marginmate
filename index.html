01

The numbers that decide a product

Everything is read from the page you are already looking at, then written as one sentence: this product makes money, or it does not.

01

Fee breakdown: Referral fee by category, FBA fulfilment by size tier, storage, inbound placement, ads and returns — each on its own line, with the rate shown.

02

Break-even price: The exact price where profit reaches zero, so you know how far a discount can go before it costs you money.

03

Target price: Set a margin and get the price to charge for this product, recalculated while you edit cost and freight.

04

Search-page margins: A profit badge on every result card and a sort by margin, so an entire niche can be scanned in seconds.

05

CSV export: Export a search page or your saved products with the full breakdown — ready for Excel, no copy-paste.

06

16 marketplaces: Fee tables and VAT handling per storefront. US ships a tiered FBA table; UK and DE cover referral rates and VAT, with the FBA fee entered manually; other storefronts fall back to the US table.

Where the numbers come from

  • Rates transcribed from Amazon's public fee schedules, versioned in one file
  • Tables refresh from a static JSON file, so rate changes need no store review
  • Every line shows the rate or size tier that produced it
  • Estimates, not promises — Amazon exceptions are yours to confirm
