What VKT MarginMate stores

Everything you type — unit cost, freight, duty, ad percentage, return rate, target margin — is written to your browser's extension storage (chrome.storage.local), together with the products you save. This data stays on your device. It is not synchronised, not transmitted, and is removed when you uninstall the extension or clear it from the popup.

What VKT MarginMate reads

On Amazon product pages the extension reads the price, ASIN, title, breadcrumb category, product dimensions and item weight, because these are the inputs of the fee calculation. On search-result pages it reads the price and ASIN of each result card to draw the margin badges. Page content is processed in memory, never recorded, never logged, never uploaded.

Network requests

  • Fee-table refresh — one daily GET to annmax1983.com/static/marginmate/fees.json, a static JSON file. No identifiers, no query parameters.
  • Licence validation — only when you activate a Pro key. The request carries the licence key, a random per-install identifier, the plugin ID and basic browser metadata (browser name and version, language, timezone, user agent) to api.annmax1983.com. It is used to bind the licence to a device and to check it is still valid.

What VKT MarginMate does not do

  • No analytics, telemetry, advertising or tracking pixels.
  • No collection of names, email addresses, payments or Seller Central credentials.
  • No upload of page content, screenshots or browsing history.
  • No sale or sharing of data with third parties.

Permissions

  • storage — keep your cost inputs and saved products locally.
  • alarms — schedule the daily fee-table refresh.
  • Amazon host access — draw the panel and the margin badges on pages you open.

Removing your data

Uninstalling the extension deletes all locally stored data. Saved products and defaults can also be cleared at any time from the popup (Clear saved products). Deactivating a licence in the popup releases the device slot on the server.
