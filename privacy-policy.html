VKT MarginMate Privacy Policy

Profit calculations run in your browser; the extension does not collect or share personal data. It reads the price, ASIN and product details of the Amazon page you have open, keeps your cost inputs in local extension storage, and contacts the licence server only when you activate an optional Pro licence. No analytics, no account.

01Data Collection

VKT MarginMate does not collect any of the following:

  • Personal identification information — no name, no email address, no payment details
  • Browsing history or the list of pages you visited
  • Usage analytics, telemetry, cookies or tracking identifiers
  • Page content — the price, ASIN and title are read into memory, never recorded and never sent anywhere
  • Screenshots, files or downloads of any kind

The extension reads only the fields a fee calculation needs, and only on the Amazon pages you open yourself: on a product page the price, ASIN, title, category from the breadcrumb, item dimensions and item weight; on a search-result page the title and price of each result card.

02Data Storage

VKT MarginMate stores the following data locally in your browser using the browser extension storage API (chrome.storage.local):

  • Cost inputs per product — unit cost, freight, duty, ad percentage, return percentage, target margin and any FBA fee you type in yourself, saved against the ASIN
  • Default inputs — the values you set in the popup, used to pre-fill every product page
  • Saved products — the ASIN, title, URL, marketplace and inputs of each product you save
  • Fee-table cache — the most recently fetched fee table and its version number
  • Licence data — the licence key you entered, its activation status, and a random identifier generated for this installation

Local data stays on your device. It is not synchronised with any account, and it is removed when you uninstall the extension or clear it from the popup.

03Permissions

  • storage — Required to keep your cost inputs, saved products and the fee-table cache on your device.
  • alarms — Required to schedule the once-a-day fee-table check. The alarm only requests the static fee file; no page data is involved.
  • Host access to the 16 Amazon marketplaces — Required to read the price, ASIN, category and dimensions of a product page and to draw the profit panel and the search-result badges. The content scripts run on those domains only.
  • Host access to annmax1983.com — Required to fetch the versioned fee table (/static/marginmate/fees.json), so a rate change does not need a new store review.
  • Host access to api.annmax1983.com — Required only to activate or validate a Pro licence.

VKT MarginMate does not use these permissions to read personal data, track your activity, or transmit page content to external servers.

04Fee Calculations

  • Every figure is computed in your browser from the fee schedules Amazon publishes.
  • The tables ship inside the extension and may be replaced by a newer copy fetched from annmax1983.com; that request carries no identifier and no query parameters.
  • The version of the table in use is shown in the panel footer and in the popup.
  • Nothing is uploaded: your unit cost, freight and duty never leave the device.

VKT MarginMate is an estimator. Amazon applies category, size, program and promotion exceptions that no product page can reveal, and rates change several times a year. Confirm the final figures in Seller Central before you price a product.

05Third-Party Services

VKT MarginMate does not use analytics or advertising services. The Amazon pages you open are requested by your own browser, and Amazon may see your IP address and request metadata under its own policy. The only connection to the developer is the licence check at api.annmax1983.com, which happens when you activate a Pro key and again when the cached licence needs revalidating. It carries the licence key, a random identifier for this installation, the plugin ID and basic browser metadata (browser name and version, language, timezone, user agent) — never page content, product data or browsing history. Our licence server records the IP address of those requests solely for rate limiting and abuse prevention, never for profiling, advertising, or sharing with third parties. Payment is handled by our checkout provider, so we never see your card details.

06Data retention & deletion

Your inputs and saved products remain in your browser until you delete them: Clear saved products in the popup removes the per-ASIN data, and uninstalling the extension removes everything it stored. Nothing is retained on our servers, because your cost data is never sent to them. Deactivating a licence releases the device slot on the licence server and deletes the key from this browser.

07Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published on this page with a new date. Material changes will be noted in the extension's release notes.
